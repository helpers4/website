helpers4 is free, open-source software (LGPL-3.0). Everything on this site is maintained in the open, and the time that goes into it — reviewing, releasing, keeping dependencies and CI healthy — is mostly unpaid. Sponsorship makes that sustainable.

## Where to give

| Platform | Best for | Link |
| -------- | -------- | ---- |
| **Open Collective** | A public ledger: anyone can see what comes in and what the money pays for. | [opencollective.com/helpers4](https://opencollective.com/helpers4) |
| **GitHub Sponsors** | Recurring support straight from your GitHub account. _Coming soon._ | — |

One-time and recurring contributions are both welcome, from individuals and from companies.

## What the money pays for

- **Maintenance time** — triage, reviews, releases, and answering issues across the four projects.
- **Infrastructure** — hosting for this site and the services behind CI (coverage, mutation testing, security scanning).
- **Keeping the bar high** — the 100% coverage gate, mutation testing, and supply-chain checks that every release goes through.

## Other ways to help

Not every contribution is financial:

- Star the repositories you use — [typescript](https://github.com/helpers4/typescript), [rust](https://github.com/helpers4/rust), [devcontainer](https://github.com/helpers4/devcontainer), [action](https://github.com/helpers4/action).
- Report a bug or propose a helper by opening an issue.
- Send a pull request — each repository has a `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
- Tell a colleague.

## Companies

If your company relies on helpers4, a sponsorship is the simplest way to say so. Reach out through [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/helpers4) and we will gladly list your logo here.
